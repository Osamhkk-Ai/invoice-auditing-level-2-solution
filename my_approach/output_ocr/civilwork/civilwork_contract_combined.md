# PART I — GENERAL CONDITIONS

### 1. Definitions

Words and expressions defined in Appendix A shall bear those meanings throughout. Where a word is used in both the singular and the plural the meaning is unaffected.

### 2. Subcontract documents

This Agreement, the General Conditions, Schedules 1 to 5 and Appendix A together constitute the whole agreement between the parties. In the event of conflict, a Schedule prevails over the General Conditions, and a later-numbered Schedule prevails over an earlier one.

### 3. Scope of the Works

The Subcontractor shall execute the civil and earthworks described in Schedule 1 at the locations shown on the Contract Drawings, together with all work reasonably to be inferred as necessary for their completion.

### 4. The Site

The Site is divided into four zones for the purposes of measurement, described in Schedule 2. The zone applicable to an item of work is the zone in which the work is physically executed, irrespective of where materials are stored or plant is based.

### 5. Ground conditions

The ground is classified into five categories described in Schedule 3. Classification shall be determined by the Engineer from the trial pit records and confirmed in writing before the work is executed. A classification once confirmed shall not be revised retrospectively.

### 6. Programme

The Subcontractor shall submit a programme within fourteen days of the Commencement Date and shall update it monthly.

### 7. Working hours

Normal working hours are 06:00 to 18:00 on working days. Work executed between 18:00 and 06:00 is Night Work and attracts the uplift stated in Schedule 4 where, and only where, that Schedule marks the item as eligible.

### 8. Rest days

The rest days under this Subcontract are Friday and Saturday. Work executed on a rest day attracts the rest-day uplift stated in Schedule 4 where the item is marked as eligible. No item attracts both the night uplift and the rest-day uplift on the same measurement unless the Engineer has instructed the work in writing under Clause 9.

### 9. Instructed out-of-hours working

The Engineer may instruct work outside normal working hours. An instruction shall be in writing and shall state whether the night uplift, the rest-day uplift, or both are to apply.

### 10. Care of the Works

The Subcontractor shall take full responsibility for the care of the Works until the date of the Taking-Over Certificate.

# PART II — MEASUREMENT AND VALUATION

### 25. Principle of measurement

The Works shall be measured net as executed in accordance with the Civil Engineering Standard Method of Measurement, as modified by these Clauses. Where a Clause of this Part conflicts with the Method of Measurement, this Part prevails.

### 26. Units of measurement

Each item in Schedule 1 states the unit in which it is measured. No item shall be measured in any other unit, and a quantity presented in a unit other than that stated shall be rejected in its entirety rather than converted.

### 27. Build-up of rates

The rates in Schedule 1 are base rates. The sum payable for a measured quantity is ascertained by applying to the base rate, in the following order and no other: first the site zone factor from Schedule 2; then the ground classification factor from Schedule 3; then the night uplift, if applicable; then the rest-day uplift, if applicable; then the cumulative quantity rebate from Schedule 4, if the threshold has been passed; and the resulting sum is then rounded.

### 28. Rounding

Rounding shall be applied once only, to the rate after the whole of the build-up described in Clause 27 has been completed, to the nearest halala, a half halala being rounded upward. Rates shall not be rounded at intermediate steps. The amount for a measured quantity is the quantity multiplied by the rounded rate; where Clause 30 divides a quantity at a rebate threshold, each part is multiplied by its own rounded rate and the amount is their sum.

### 29. Application of factors

A factor applies to an item only where Schedule 2 or Schedule 3 lists that item's series. An item whose series is not listed is measured at the base rate irrespective of the zone or ground classification in which it is executed.

### 30. Cumulative quantity rebates

Where Schedule 4 states a rebate threshold for an item, the rebate applies to that part of the quantity which exceeds the threshold when taken cumulatively across the whole of the Works to date, and not to the quantity below it. The cumulative count runs from the Commencement Date, is not reset by a valuation, and is taken in order of the date of execution; measurements executed on the same date are taken in order of application number and then line number.

### 31. Daily limitations

Where Schedule 4 states a daily limitation for an item, no more than that quantity is measurable for that item, per work area, per day. A quantity in excess of the limitation is not payable and shall not be carried forward to a subsequent day.

### 32. Mutually exclusive items

Where Schedule 4 states that an item is excluded by another, the excluded item is not measurable within the stated number of days following the measurement of the excluding item on the same work area.

# PART III — PAYMENT, RETENTION AND RECORDS

### 40. Interim valuations

The Subcontractor shall submit a payment application monthly. Each application shall state the period of the Works it covers, being the first and last day on which any measured item included in it was executed.

### 41. Period of an application

An application covers a period which has closed. No application shall be submitted before the last day of the period it states, and no item shall be included in an application whose stated period does not include the day on which the work was executed. An application shall be submitted within 21 days of the end of the period it covers.

### 42. Content of an application

Each application shall list every item measured, stating the item code, the date of execution, the Site zone, the ground classification where applicable, the quantity, the unit, the built-up rate applied and the resulting amount.

### 43. Arithmetic

The total of an application shall equal the sum of the amounts of the items listed in it. An application whose total does not so agree shall be returned unpaid.

### 44. Duplicate measurement

No item shall be measured twice. Where the same item, work area and date appears more than once in an application, or in more than one application, the later measurement shall be disallowed in full.

### 45. Retention

The Contractor shall retain 5 per cent of the value of each valuation. Retention is calculated on the total of the application and not on individual items, and is rounded down to the nearest halala.

### 46. Records as a condition of payment

Where Schedule 5 states that a record is required for an item, the item is not payable in any valuation until that record has been delivered to the Engineer. The absence of a required record is a breach of this Clause and not merely a want of corroboration.

### 47. Form of records

A record required by Schedule 5 shall identify the work area, the date, the item code and the quantity, and shall be signed by the Subcontractor's foreman and countersigned by the Engineer's representative.

### 48. Payment period

The Contractor shall pay a correctly submitted application within thirty days of its receipt.

### 49. Set-off

The Contractor may set off against any sum due amounts properly due to it under this Subcontract.

### 50. Final account

The final account shall be submitted within ninety days of the Taking-Over Certificate.

# PART IV — SPECIFICATION PREAMBLES

### S1. Setting out and control

Primary control stations are as shown on drawing CW-4-SU-001. The Subcontractor shall verify the stations before commencing and shall report any discrepancy exceeding 5 mm in plan or 3 mm in level.

### S2. Topsoil strip

Topsoil shall be stripped to the full depth of the organic horizon and stockpiled separately in the location shown. Stripping to a depth greater than that instructed shall not be measured.

### S3. Excavation tolerances

Formation levels shall be within plus or minus 20 mm of the levels shown. Over-excavation shall be made good with imported granular fill at the Subcontractor's cost and shall not be measured under item A.14.010.

### S4. Classification of excavated material

Material shall be classified at the point of excavation and recorded on the daily excavation record. A classification not recorded on the day of excavation shall be taken to be G2 Firm Sabkha.

### S5. Disposal

Material classified as unsuitable shall be removed to a licensed tip. Tip tickets shall accompany the payment application in which the disposal is measured.

### S6. Compaction

Granular fill shall be placed in layers not exceeding 225 mm loose depth and compacted to 95 per cent of the maximum dry density. One compaction test shall be carried out per 500 m2 per layer.

### S7. Concrete materials

Cement shall be sulphate-resisting Portland cement. Aggregate shall be from an approved source and shall be tested for chloride content monthly.

### S8. Concrete placing

Concrete shall not be placed when the shade air temperature exceeds 38 degrees Celsius without the written approval of the Engineer. Approval for placing at night does not of itself constitute an instruction under Clause 9.

### S9. Curing

All concrete shall be cured for not less than seven days by an approved method. Curing is deemed included in the rates.

### S10. Formwork

Formwork shall be of a quality producing the finish described. Formwork to soffits shall not be struck before the concrete has attained seventy-five per cent of its characteristic strength.

### S11. Reinforcement

Reinforcement shall be measured by the theoretical mass of the bar schedule. Laps not shown on the schedule and tying wire, spacers and chairs are deemed included and are not separately measurable.

### S12. Drainage bedding

Pipes shall be bedded in Class B granular bedding unless otherwise shown. Bedding is deemed included in the pipe rates.

### S13. Pressure testing

Pressure pipework shall be tested in accordance with the Specification and the certificate delivered before the pipework is included in a valuation.

### S14. Manholes

Precast manhole rings shall be bedded on a mortar joint. The depth measured shall be from finished cover level to the invert of the outgoing pipe.

### S15. Sub-base

Type 1 granular sub-base shall be laid and compacted in a single layer where the compacted thickness does not exceed 250 mm.

### S16. Bituminous materials

Bituminous materials shall be laid at a temperature not less than 130 degrees Celsius and shall not be laid in standing water.

### S17. Surface course tolerances

The finished surface shall be within plus or minus 6 mm of the design level when tested with a 3 m straight edge.

### S18. Kerbs

Kerbs shall be bedded on a concrete foundation and haunched to the detail shown. The foundation and haunching are deemed included.

### S19. Road marking

Thermoplastic material shall be applied to a clean dry surface. Marking applied to a surface course less than fourteen days old shall be re-applied at the Subcontractor's cost.

### S20. Adverse weather

Work shall be suspended when rainfall exceeds 5 mm in any hour. A suspension shall be recorded on the meteorological record, and standby under item E.51.010 is measurable only for the hours so recorded.

# PART V — PARTICULAR CONDITIONS

### P1. Precedence of this Part

This Part amends the General Conditions. Where a provision of this Part conflicts with Parts I to III, this Part prevails.

### P2. Project-specific working restrictions

No work shall be executed in Zone Z3 Wadi Crossing during a declared flood watch. Standby during a flood watch is measurable under item E.51.010 and not under item E.51.030.

### P3. Access to the Escarpment platform

Zone Z4 Escarpment is served by a single-track haul road. The Subcontractor shall programme deliveries to avoid simultaneous movements and shall not claim standing time arising from failure to do so.

### P4. Restrictions on night working

Night work in Zone Z1 Compound requires the written consent of the Contractor by reason of the proximity of occupied accommodation. Consent does not of itself entitle the Subcontractor to the night uplift, which is payable only on the items marked in Schedule 4.

### P5. Existing services

The Subcontractor shall carry out a service scan before any excavation and shall record the result. Damage to a service shown on the record is at the Subcontractor's cost.

### P6. Statutory undertakers

Attendance on a statutory undertaker is measured under item E.53.010 and is limited to two days per work area per day of attendance irrespective of the number of undertakers attending.

### P7. Archaeological finds

Work shall cease immediately on the discovery of a find of archaeological interest and shall not resume without the written instruction of the Engineer.

### P8. Dust suppression

Dust suppression is deemed included in the rates and is not separately measurable, save that water bowser standing time during a declared dust event is measurable under item E.51.030.

### P9. Working in high temperatures

Work shall be suspended between 12:00 and 15:00 during the period from 15 June to 15 September in accordance with the applicable labour regulations. Such a suspension is not a ground for standby.

### P10. Ramadan working

Working hours during Ramadan shall be as agreed in writing before the commencement of the month. An agreed variation of hours does not of itself make work Night Work.

### P11. Concurrent uplifts

# PART VI — HEALTH, SAFETY AND ENVIRONMENT

### H1. Health and safety management

The Subcontractor shall appoint a full-time safety officer for the duration of the Works and shall maintain a safety file available for inspection at all times.

### H2. Method statements and risk assessments

No activity shall commence before an approved method statement and risk assessment have been accepted by the Engineer. Work executed without an accepted method statement shall not be measured.

### H3. Permits to work

A permit to work is required for excavation exceeding 1.2 m in depth, for any work in a confined space, for hot work, and for work within 3 m of a live service.

### H4. Excavation support

No person shall enter an excavation exceeding 1.2 m in depth unless the sides are supported or battered to a safe angle. Earthwork support is measured under items A.15.010 and A.15.020.

### H5. Personal protective equipment

Safety helmet, safety footwear, high-visibility clothing and eye protection shall be worn at all times on the Site.

### H6. Heat stress

The Subcontractor shall implement the heat stress management plan, including the midday break required by Clause P9 and the provision of shaded rest areas and drinking water.

### H7. Lifting operations

All lifting operations shall be planned by an appointed person and supervised by a competent lift supervisor.

### H8. Traffic and pedestrian segregation

Vehicle routes and pedestrian routes shall be physically segregated. A banksman shall be provided for every reversing movement.

### H9. Accident reporting

Any accident shall be reported to the Contractor within one hour and in writing within twenty-four hours.

### H10. Environmental management

The Subcontractor shall comply with the Project Environmental Management Plan and shall not discharge any substance to ground or watercourse.

### H11. Waste

Waste shall be segregated and removed to a licensed facility. Waste transfer notes shall be retained for the duration of the Works and produced on request.

### H12. Noise

# PART VII — PARTICULAR CONDITIONS (SECOND SERIES)

### 3A. Contract Years

The first Contract Year runs from the Commencement Date to the day before its first anniversary. Each following Contract Year runs from that anniversary. The final Contract Year ends on the Date for Completion as extended by any Amendment, however long that leaves it; an extension of time does not begin a new Contract Year.

### 6A. Chargeable Hour

Where an item is measured by the hour, a Chargeable Hour is an hour of attendance other than the first hour of that attendance. The site record states the hours attended; the quantity measured is one hour fewer.

### 26A. Rates stated in another currency

Where Schedule 1 states a rate in USD, the rate is converted at the rate of exchange published in Schedule 2B for the calendar month in which the work was executed, and the converted figure is rounded to the nearest halala with a half rounded to the nearer even halala. The conversion is made before any factor or uplift is applied.

### 27A. Conditions on the uplifts and factors

The night-working uplift is not payable on an item priced at a zone factor exceeding 1.1, the allowance for working away from the compound being taken to include it. In any period of extension of time granted by an Amendment, that is to say for work executed after 27 September 2025, the ground classification is taken as G2 Firm Sabkha whatever the Engineer recorded on the day.

### 29A. Indexed rates

The rate for an item listed in Schedule 2A is the base rate stated in Schedule 1 multiplied by the Site Materials Index published for the calendar month in which the work was executed and divided by the base index, and rounded to the nearest halala with a half rounded to the nearer even halala. That figure is then treated as the rate for the purposes of Clause 27, and Clause 28 applies to the build-up as usual.

### 31A. Instruments taking effect before their date of issue

Where an Amendment substitutes a rate with effect from a date before the date on which it was issued, an Application for Payment submitted before that date of issue is not thereby incorrect: it was valued at the rate then in force. The difference between the rates on work already valued shall be shown as a single adjustment on the first Application for Payment submitted on or after the date of issue, and on no other.

### 33A. Tolerance on measurement

Where an item is measured against a joint survey, a measured quantity not exceeding the surveyed quantity by more than 2 per cent is payable as measured. A measured quantity exceeding the surveyed quantity by more than that is payable at the surveyed quantity only.

### 45A. Release of retention

On the first Application for Payment submitted after the Date for Completion as extended, one half of the retention held on all earlier Applications is released, rounded down to the halala. The amount payable on an Application is the measured total, plus any adjustment under Clause 31A, less the retention deducted on it, plus any retention released under this Clause.
