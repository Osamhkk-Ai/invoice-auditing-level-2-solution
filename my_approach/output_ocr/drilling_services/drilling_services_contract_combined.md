# PART I — GENERAL CONDITIONS

**1. Definitions**

Words defined in Appendix A have those meanings throughout.

**2. Contract documents**

This Contract comprises this Agreement, Parts I to VII, Schedules 1 to 6 and Appendices A to C. A Schedule prevails over a Part, and Part VI prevails over Parts I to V.

**3. Scope**

The Contractor shall provide the services described in Schedule 1 on each well the Company nominates by call-off, from spud to release of the drilling rig from that well.

**4. Call-off**

Each well is called off by a written notice stating the well name, the rig, the field and the well class. The well class stated in the call-off governs the whole well and is not revised while the well is drilled.

**5. Term**

The Contract runs from the Commencement Date to the Expiry Date.

**6. Contractor's personnel**

The Contractor shall provide the personnel in Schedule 4, competent and certified for the work, on a rotation that keeps the service continuous.

**7. Contractor's equipment**

The Contractor shall provide the tools in Schedule 1 in good order, inspected and certified, with backup tools at the rig site where Part V requires them.

**8. Company-provided items**

The Company provides the drilling rig, drilling fluids, bits, casing and accommodation, and transport of the Contractor's equipment between wells within the same field.

**9. Instructions**

The Contractor shall act on the written instructions of the Company Representative.

**10. Independent contractor**

The Contractor is an independent contractor.

**11. Liabilities**

Each party is responsible for injury to its own personnel and loss of its own property, save as Clause 31 provides for tools lost in the hole.

**12. Confidentiality**

# PART II — OPERATIONS AND FIELD RECORDS

**15. Daily Drilling Report**

A Daily Drilling Report shall be issued for every day on which the Contractor is on the well. It records the well, the rig, the date, the hole section, the measured depth at the start and end of the day, the status of the day as Operating or Standby, the circulating hours, the BHA run number, the tools in the hole, the Contractor's personnel on the rig, and the number of gyro surveys, formation pressure points, wiper trips, back-reaming hours and clean-out runs. It is signed by the Company Representative and the Contractor's lead directional driller.

**16. Other field records**

A BHA Run Report is issued at the end of each BHA run and records the run number, the hole section, the first and last day of the run, the tools in the run, the circulating hours, the metres logged and the metres reamed. A Gyro Survey Report is issued for each day on which a gyro survey is taken, a Source Handling Certificate for each run carrying a radioactive source, and a Lost in Hole Report for each tool that cannot be recovered.

# PART III — PRICING

**17. Rounding**

Every amount is ascertained in cents. Where a step of the build-up in Clause 18 produces a fraction of a cent, the result of that step is rounded to the nearest cent before the next step is applied, and a result exactly half way between two cents is rounded to the even cent.

**18. Build-up of rates**

The rate for a charge is the Schedule 1 rate, or for item PD-210 the Schedule 2 rate, to which is applied, in this order and no other: first the hole section factor in Schedule 3 Part 1, where the service is section-rated; then the well class factor in Schedule 3 Part 2, where the service is class-rated; then the standby percentage in Schedule 3 Part 3, where the day is a Standby day. Each step is rounded under Clause 17. The amount for the charge is the quantity multiplied by the rate so built up.

**19. Status of a day**

The status of a day, Operating or Standby, and the hole section for that day are as recorded on the Daily Drilling Report for that day. A charge shall state the status and the hole section of the day it is for.

**20. Standby**

On a Standby day a service is charged at the percentage of its operating rate stated in Schedule 3 Part 3. A service Schedule 3 Part 3 marks as not chargeable is not charged on a Standby day in any quantity.

**21. Circulating hours**

Item DD-120 is charged per circulating hour recorded on the Daily Drilling Report for an Operating day, subject to a minimum of 6 hours on any Operating day on which the tool is in the hole. On a Standby day item DD-120 is not charged and item DD-121 is charged instead.

**22. Personnel and daily limits**

Personnel are charged per person-day for the persons recorded on the rig on the Daily Drilling Report. No service is charged on any day in a quantity above the daily limit in Schedule 3 Part 5.

**23. Performance drilling footage**

Item PD-210 is charged for the metres drilled on the day, taken from the measured depths on the Daily Drilling Report, at the rate of the Schedule 2 depth band in which the metres lie. Where the metres drilled on a day cross a band boundary, the metres in each band are charged as a separate charge stating the depths at which that part starts and ends. A boundary depth belongs to the shallower band. Bands end at 1,500 m, 3,000 m and 4,500 m. Item PD-210 is charged only on the performance-drilled sections nominated in the call-off.

**24. Metres logged**

Items LW-410, LW-411 and LW-412 are charged for the metres drilled on an Operating day while the tool is in the hole, which are the metres logged.

**25. Metres reamed**

Item RM-510 is charged for the metres drilled on an Operating day while the underreamer is in the hole, which are the metres reamed.

Gyro surveys, formation pressure points, wiper trips, back-reaming hours and clean-out runs are charged in the numbers recorded on the Daily Drilling Report for an Operating day.

**31. Lost in hole**

Where a tool cannot be recovered, the charge is its replacement value in Schedule 6 less depreciation of 1 per cent for each complete 25 circulating hours the tool accumulated on the well, as stated on the Lost in Hole Report, to a depreciation of not more than 50 per cent. The result is rounded under Clause 17 and charged as one unit on the day of the loss.

# PART IV — INVOICING AND PAYMENT

**32. Invoices**

The Contractor shall invoice each well separately, for a period of consecutive days on the well. Every charge on an invoice is for a day within the period the invoice states.

**33. Submission**

An invoice shall not be submitted before the last day of its period and shall be submitted within 30 days after it.

**34. Content of a charge**

Each charge states the date, the service code, the description, the hole section, the status of the day, the quantity, the unit, the rate and the amount, and for item PD-210 the depths at which the charged interval starts and ends.

**35. Units**

A charge is stated in the unit Schedule 1 gives for the service, and in no other.

**36. Net amount**

The net amount of an invoice is the sum of the amounts of its charges, including the discount charge under Clause 38.

**37. Documents as a condition of payment**

A charge for a service Schedule 5 lists is not payable until the document Schedule 5 names has been delivered for the day or run the charge is for.

**38. Discount**

Where the sum of the amounts of the services charged on an invoice exceeds 250,000.00 USD, the Contractor allows a discount of 4 per cent of the excess, rounded under Clause 17, stated as a single charge under code DS-900 with a negative amount.

**39. Value Added Tax**

Value Added Tax is charged at 15 per cent of the net amount of the invoice, rounded under Clause 17.

**40. Invoice total**

The total of an invoice is its net amount plus Value Added Tax.

**41. Disputed charges**

The Company may withhold a disputed charge and shall pay the undisputed balance.

**42. Payment**

The Company shall pay within forty-five days of receipt of a correct invoice.

# PART V — TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

**T1. Survey programme**

Surveys shall be taken at intervals not exceeding 30 m in any build or turn section and 90 m in tangent sections.

**T2. Survey accuracy**

MWD surveys shall meet the ISCWSA error model for the tool; gyro surveys shall be run where magnetic interference is expected.

**T3. Anti-collision**

No section shall be drilled without an approved anti-collision report for the well.

**T4. Rotary steerable systems**

Rotary steerable systems shall be capable of dog-leg severities of 6 degrees per 30 m in 12-1/4 inch and 8-1/2 inch hole.

**T5. Positive displacement motors**

Motors shall be inspected and redressed after every run; redress is charged under Clause 26.

**T6. MWD tools**

MWD tools shall transmit inclination, azimuth, tool face and gamma ray in real time.

**T7. Pressure while drilling**

The pressure while drilling module shall record annular and bore pressure at intervals not exceeding 10 seconds.

**T8. LWD resistivity**

Resistivity shall be measured at no fewer than four depths of investigation.

**T9. LWD density and neutron**

Density and neutron measurements require a radioactive source handled under Clause H6.

**T10. LWD sonic**

The sonic tool shall record compressional and shear slowness.

**T11. Formation pressure testing**

Pressure points shall be taken only at depths the Company Representative approves.

**T12. Underreaming**

The underreamer shall open the hole to not less than the diameter in the well programme and shall be verified by caliper.

**T13. Hole cleaning**

Cuttings bed monitoring shall be provided in 12-1/4 inch hole with inclination above 60 degrees.

**T14. Jars**

# PART VI — PARTICULAR CONDITIONS

**P1. Precedence**

This Part amends Parts I to V and prevails over them.

**P2. High pressure, high temperature wells**

A well is HPHT where the call-off so states; the class-rated factor applies to the whole well and not only to the deeper sections.

**P3. Extended reach wells**

A well is Extended Reach where the call-off so states, whatever the horizontal displacement achieved.

**P4. Rig moves**

No service is charged for a day on which the rig is moving between wells; such days are not days on the well.

**P5. Weather**

A day lost to weather is recorded as Standby on the Daily Drilling Report.

**P6. Tool failure**

Where a tool fails in the hole, the day remains an Operating day for the other services; the failed tool is charged only on days the report records it in the hole.

**P7. Crew changes**

A person on a crew change day is counted once, whether arriving or departing.

**P8. Night working**

No uplift is payable for work at night; the rates in Schedule 1 cover continuous operations.

**P9. Public holidays**

No uplift is payable for work on a public holiday.

**P10. Minimum hours**

The minimum in Clause 21 applies to each Operating day separately and is not averaged over a run.

**P11. Discount basis**

The discount under Clause 38 is calculated on each invoice alone and not on the Contractor's invoices taken together.

**P12. Lost in hole hours**

Circulating hours for Clause 31 are those the tool accumulated on the well on which it was lost, including the day of the loss.

**P13. Standby personnel**

Personnel are charged on a Standby day at the standby percentage for their position in Schedule 3 Part 3.

**H1. Compliance**

# PART VIII — REPORTING AND DATA

**R1. Reporting responsibility**

The lead directional driller is responsible for the accuracy of the Contractor's entries on the Daily Drilling Report.

**R2. Time of issue**

The Daily Drilling Report for a day is issued by 06:00 on the following day.

**R3. Depths**

Measured depths are stated in whole metres from the rotary table.

**R4. Circulating hours**

Circulating hours are stated in whole hours, any part hour of thirty minutes or more counting as a whole hour.

**R5. Tools in the hole**

The report lists by service code every rental tool in the hole at any time during the day.

**R6. Personnel**

The report states, by service code, the number of the Contractor's personnel on the rig during the day.

**R7. Status**

The status of the day is recorded by the Company Representative and is final for the purposes of Clause 19.

**R8. Corrections**

A report may be corrected only by a revision signed by both signatories before the invoice for the day is submitted.

**R9. BHA Run Report**

The BHA Run Report is issued within two days of the end of the run and totals the circulating hours and metres of the Daily Drilling Reports for the run.

**R10. Survey data**

Survey data are delivered in the Company's format within twelve hours of each survey.

**R11. Log data**

LWD data are delivered in real time where the tools allow and in memory form at the end of each run.

**R12. Retention of records**

The Contractor shall retain all field records for seven years after the end of the Contract.

# PART IX — PARTICULAR CONDITIONS (SECOND SERIES)

**3A. Contract Years**

The first Contract Year runs from the Commencement Date to the day before its first anniversary, and each following Contract Year from that anniversary. The final Contract Year ends on the Expiry Date as extended by any Amendment; an extension does not begin a new Contract Year.

**17A. Indexed rates**

The rate for a service listed in Schedule 2C is the base rate stated in Schedule 1 multiplied by the Rig Services Index published for the calendar month in which the service was performed and divided by the base index, rounded half to even. That figure is the rate for the purposes of Clause 17.

**17B. Factors that do not apply**

The hole-section factor in Schedule 3 Part 1 is not applied on a day the rig is on Standby, the section not being drilled. The well-class factor in Schedule 3 Part 2 is not applied to item PD-210, Schedule 2 having already priced the metre by depth.

**19A. The Daily Drilling Report and its number**

Each Daily Drilling Report bears a number in the series DDR, and that number is quoted against every line of that day on the invoice. The report is written by the Contractor's supervisor in the ordinary words of the rig and does not repeat the descriptions in Schedule 1 or carry the service codes.

**21A. Chargeable Hour**

For a service charged by the hour, the first hour of each period in the hole is rig-up and is not a Chargeable Hour. The report states the hours run; the invoice charges one fewer.

**25A. Tolerance on metres**

Metres charged not exceeding the metres the report supports by more than 1 per cent are payable as charged. Metres exceeding that are payable at the figure the report supports.

**31A. Replacement values**

The replacement values in Schedule 2D are stated in SAR and are converted into the currency of the Contract at the rate of exchange published in that Schedule for the month in which the tool was lost, rounded half to even, before the depreciation in Clause 31 is applied. Schedule 6 states the same values in the currency of the Contract as they stood when it was let, and Schedule 2D governs.

**36A. Amendments taking effect before their date of issue**

Where an Amendment substitutes a rate with effect from a date before the date it was issued, an invoice submitted before that date of issue was correct at the rate then in force. The difference on services already invoiced is shown as a single adjustment on the first invoice submitted on or after the date of issue, and on no other.
