   The full descriptions appear on pages 15 and 16 (Schedule 1). Not corrected in the page files.

Transcribed as printed; flagging so the audit does not assume the contents page is exhaustive.
4. **Multi-column tables that were flattened.** These were transcribed into Markdown tables and each cell was checked, but they carry the most risk of a row or column slip:
   - Page 26, Schedule 7 tool specifications (5 columns, 20 rows, several wrapped tool names).
   - Pages 18 and 19, two-column month/value tables (24 values each).
   - Pages 20 to 22, Schedule 3 standby percentages and daily limits.
   - Page 30, Appendix B form of invoice (8 columns).
5. **Dates and effective dates on pages 37 to 42.** Amendment No. 3 is issued 2026-08-17 but effective 2026-02-01, and the Schedule of Variations on page 37 shows the same. This is transcribed as printed; it is a feature of the contract (retroactive amendment under Clause 36A), not a transcription error.
